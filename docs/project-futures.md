# Project Futures (Ideas Only)

> Last updated: 2026-04-14

## 1) Regulation-backed eligibility rules engine
- **Value:** Replace static heuristics with policy rules mapped to Mexican lender and regulatory constraints.
- **Cost:** Medium–High.
- **Risk:** Rules can become stale if updates are not maintained.
- **Validation:** Compare recommendations vs. historical underwriter outcomes.

## 2) Explainable recommendations panel
- **Value:** Show top factors improving or hurting eligibility and next steps (e.g., reduce debt ratio).
- **Cost:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Could be misread as formal financial advice.
- **Validation:** User comprehension survey + lower form abandonment.

## 3) Consent + secure lead capture workflow
- **Value:** Turn pre-qualifications into compliant sales pipeline handoffs.
- **Cost:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Privacy/compliance obligations for personal data.
- **Validation:** Lead conversion rate and consent completion metrics.
